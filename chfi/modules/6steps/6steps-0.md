### **THE CORE SHIFT: FROM "WHAT HAPPENED?" TO "HOW DO I PROVE IT?"** 🔄⚡

This isn't about finding clues—it's about building a **bulletproof case** that would hold up in a courtroom. The mindset shift is everything:

**Prevention Mindset:** "How do I stop the bad thing?"  
**Forensic Mindset:** "The bad thing happened. **Now, how do I prove who did it, how they did it, and what they took?**"

## **THE 6-STEP FORENSIC HEARTBEAT** 💓🔍

**1. 🎯 IDENTIFICATION**  
_Recognize the incident. Define the scope._  
`>> First question: "Is this a crime scene?"`

**2. 🛡️ PRESERVATION**  
_Secure the scene. Do no harm. Freeze everything._  
`>> Golden rule: Never touch the original evidence.`

**3. 📦 COLLECTION**  
_Gather the evidence. Bit-for-bit forensic image._  
`>> Create the perfect digital copy. Document everything.`

**4. 🔎 EXAMINATION**  
_Process the data. Find the patterns and artifacts._  
`>> Sift through logs, filesystems, memory. Hunt for clues.`

**5. 🧩 ANALYSIS**  
_Interpret the findings. Connect the dots._  
`>> Answer: "What happened? How? Who?" Build the story.`

**6. 📜 PRESENTATION**  
_Report the facts. Court-ready narrative._  
`>> Make complex technical truth simple for a jury.`
