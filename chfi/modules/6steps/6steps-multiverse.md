## **THE UNBREAKABLE FRAMEWORK** 💎🔧

**Regardless of field, scope, or scale—the 6-step forensic heartbeat remains constant:**

1.  **🎯 IDENTIFICATION** → "Is this a crime scene?"
2.  **🛡️ PRESERVATION** → "Freeze everything. Do no harm."
3.  **📦 COLLECTION** → "Gather the evidence systematically."
4.  **🔎 EXAMINATION** → "Find the patterns and artifacts."
5.  **🧩 ANALYSIS** → "Connect the dots. Build the story."
6.  **📜 PRESENTATION** → "Make the truth undeniable."

**What changes isn't the process—it's the context, tools, and domain knowledge required.**

## **THE PATTERN: WHAT ACTUALLY CHANGES?** 🧩⚡

### **TOOLING ADAPTATION** 🛠️
- **Traditional:** FTK, EnCase, Wireshark
- **Cloud:** Cloud-specific APIs and logging systems
- **Quantum:** Quantum measurement and state preservation tools
- **The principle remains:** Use the right tool to preserve and examine evidence without alteration.

### **DOMAIN KNOWLEDGE REQUIREMENTS** 🧠
- **Traditional:** File systems, network protocols, operating systems
- **AI:** Machine learning models, training data integrity, algorithmic fairness
- **Quantum:** Quantum mechanics, entanglement, superposition principles
- **The principle remains:** Understand the system you're investigating at a fundamental level.

### **EVIDENCE CHARACTERISTICS** 📊
- **Traditional:** Persistent on disk, measurable in bytes
- **Cloud:** Ephemeral, API-based, distributed across regions
- **Quantum:** Probabilistic, collapses upon measurement
- **The principle remains:** Evidence must be preserved in its most authentic state.

### **TIMESCALE CONSTRAINTS** ⏳
- **Traditional:** Days to weeks for disk evidence
- **Cloud:** Minutes to hours before logs rotate
- **Quantum:** Nanoseconds before decoherence
- **Quantum:** The principle remains: Collect the most volatile evidence first.
