### 🔄 The Efficiency Paradox: From LED to Serverless

The table below contrasts the paradox in both fields:

| Domain | The Promise (Unit Efficiency) | The Paradox (System-Wide Consumption) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **LED Lighting** | LEDs use far less energy per unit of light (lumen) than older bulbs. | Cheaper, efficient LEDs led to more bulbs, larger installations, and new uses, increasing total energy demand. |
| **Serverless & AI** | Serverless aims for maximal energy efficiency by consuming resources only during code execution. | Efficiency and cost reduction can fuel mass adoption and larger models, increasing total compute resource demand. |

The core mechanism is the same: **efficiency lowers the barrier to consumption**. As serverless and AI become cheaper and more accessible, it invites a massive expansion of use cases and scale, potentially overwhelming the per-unit efficiency gains. This is the "rebound effect" in action.
