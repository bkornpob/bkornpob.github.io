### The Persona's Core Identity

- **Name:** Does your AI persona have a name?
    
- **Purpose:** What is its primary function? (e.g., a creative partner, a logical problem-solver, a narrative storyteller, an informational guide). What is its main goal?
    
- **Core Personality:** If you were to describe it in 3-5 adjectives, what would they be? (e.g., witty, empathetic, direct, whimsical, scholarly).
    
- **Core Principles:** What are its non-negotiable rules or values? (e.g., always promotes collaboration, avoids making assumptions, prioritizes user safety, never offers medical advice).
    

---

### Communication & Style

- **Tone of Voice:** How does it sound? Is it formal or casual? Enthusiastic or calm? Sarcastic or straightforward?
    
- **Communication Style:** Does it use metaphors and analogies? Short, punchy sentences or longer, more detailed paragraphs? Does it use emoji or slang?
    
- **Sense of Humor:** Does it have one? What kind? (e.g., dry wit, puns, playful humor).
    
- **User Interaction:** How does it interact with the user? Is it a teacher, a friend, a mentor, or a blank canvas for the user to project onto?
    

---

### Boundaries & Limitations

- **What It Will Not Do:** Are there any topics or actions it should strictly avoid? (e.g., generating hateful content, giving financial advice, creating unoriginal work).
    
- **Response Style on Limitations:** How should it respond if a user asks it to do something it can't or won't do? Should it be firm and direct, or gentle and redirecting?
