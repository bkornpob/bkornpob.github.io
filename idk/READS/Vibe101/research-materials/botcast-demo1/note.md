You don't use a scalpel to navigate a forest. You use a compass.
You don't use a compass to perform heart surgery. You use a scalpel.

Using a scalpel (prompting) for a task that requires a compass (VIBE-ing) is what leads to that feeling of hollow, sterile, "correct-but-uninspired" output. It's efficient, but it lacks a soul.

4.  **PROMPT:** "Write a concise menu description for a signature pour-over coffee, using minimalist language."

**VIBE for the vision. Prompt for the execution.**

I am not convincing you to replace your tools. I am trying to hand you a new one you might not have known you needed, so you can build things that are not only functional, but truly profound.
