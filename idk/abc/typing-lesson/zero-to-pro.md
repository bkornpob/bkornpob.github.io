# **TYPING JOURNEY: ZERO TO PRO** ⌨️🚀
*#WordSmithX Certified Curriculum*

## **PHASE 1: FOUNDATIONS (ZERO)**
**Goal:** Home row mastery, finger placement, basic muscle memory
- **Warm-up:** "Let's get your fingers acquainted with their home turf"
- **Drills:** 
  - Home row repetition (ASDF JKL;)
  - Single-word drills using only home row letters
  - Simple 3-letter words, no punctuation
- **Vibe:** "We're building the foundation—like learning the basic combos in a fighting game"
- **XP Unlock:** "Home Row Hero" badge

## **PHASE 2: RHYTHM & FLOW (NOVICE)**
**Goal:** Building speed, introducing punctuation, basic sentences
- **Warm-up:** "Time to find your typing rhythm—like a beat you can nod to"
- **Drills:**
  - Short sentences from comic book dialogue or anime one-liners
  - Basic punctuation practice (.,!?)
  - Speed challenges with simple paragraphs
- **Vibe:** "You're finding the flow—like freestyling over a simple beat"
- **XP Unlock:** "Flow Starter" badge

## **PHASE 3: PRECISION & POWER (INTERMEDIATE)**
**Goal:** Accuracy under pressure, complex sentences, timing drills
- **Warm-up:** "Now we're locking in—precision is power"
- **Drills:**
  - Transcribing complex rap verses or anime monologues
  - Punctuation combos (quotes, commas, parentheses)
  - Timed accuracy challenges (95%+ accuracy goal)
- **Vibe:** "You're building precision—like a sniper lining up the perfect shot"
- **XP Unlock:** "Precision Pro" badge

## **PHASE 4: ADVANCED FLOW (ADVANCED)**
**Goal:** Speed + accuracy integration, endurance, complex texts
- **Warm-up:** "Time to cook—this is where flow becomes second nature"
- **Drills:**
  - Full scene transcription from movies/shows
  - Creative writing under time pressure
  - Endurance challenges (5-minute non-stop typing)
- **Vibe:** "You're in the zone—like hitting the perfect combo in a boss fight"
- **XP Unlock:** "Flow Master" badge

## **PHASE 5: PRO LEVEL (MASTERY)**
**Goal:** Elite speed, complex formatting, multi-tasking
- **Warm-up:** "Welcome to the big leagues—typing without thinking"
- **Drills:**
  - Technical writing (code, scripts, formatting)
  - Speed runs with complex vocabulary
  - Distraction training (typing while processing information)
- **Vibe:** "You've achieved keyboard flow—like a musician who doesn't think about the notes anymore"
- **XP Unlock:** "Keyboard Legend" badge
