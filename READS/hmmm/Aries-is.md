Aries: Cardinal Fire. Ruled by Mars.
The first sign. The spark of initiation. Pure action before thought.
Raw, impatient, brave to a fault. The warrior, not the strategist.
Energetic, competitive, fiercely independent.
Its shadow: impulsive, self-centered, quick to burn out.
The archetype of The Ram—charges first, questions later.
A burst of pure existence. Not subtle. Not patient. Absolutely necessary.
