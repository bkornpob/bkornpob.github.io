### **How to Give a Vibe: A Quick Guide**

A strong "vibe" combines several elements:

*   **🎯 GOAL:** What is the concrete objective? (e.g., "a summary," "a logo," "a plan")

*   **📣 TONE:** What is the voice? (e.g., "reassuring," "authoritative," "witty," "casual")
