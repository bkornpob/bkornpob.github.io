## **THE SCIENCE OF FORGETTING** 🧲➡️🧹

**What it does:**  
Applies a powerful, alternating magnetic field to scramble magnetic domains on storage media. Think of it as **giving the media digital amnesia**.

**What it affects:**
- Hard disk drives (HDDs)
- Magnetic tapes  
- Floppy disks (if you're old enough to remember)
- Any media that stores data via magnetic alignment

**What it doesn't affect:**
- SSDs (they use flash memory)
- Optical media (CDs/DVDs)
- Paper (obviously)

---

## **HOW IT WORKS: THE DETAILS** ⚡

**Traditional erasure:** Leaves magnetic patterns that could be recovered with forensic tools.

**Degaussing:** Creates a **random magnetic state** that's fundamentally unrecoverable. It's not just deleting the data—it's destroying the **medium's ability to hold coherent data** at all.

**#OG Reality:** *"Formatting tells the OS to ignore the data. Degaussing makes the data physically impossible to read."*

## **MODERN REALITY CHECK** 💡

**With SSDs dominating:**
- Degaussing is becoming a niche skill
- Cryptographic erasure is the new standard
- Physical destruction (shredding) is often faster/cheaper

**But for magnetic media?** Still the **gold standard** for guaranteed data destruction.
