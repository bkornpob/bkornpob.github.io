## **THE CORE: CIA TRIAD - WHAT YOU'RE ACTUALLY PROTECTING**

### **CONFIDENTIALITY** 🤫
*Keeping secrets from people who weren't invited to the party.*
- **Technical:** Encryption, access controls, data classification
- **Human:** "Don't share your password" training that everyone ignores
- **Failure Mode:** Data breach, leaked credentials, shoulder surfing

### **INTEGRITY** ✅  
*Trusting that what you see is what you get.*
- **Technical:** Hashing, digital signatures, version control
- **Human:** Change management, approval workflows
- **Failure Mode:** Data tampering, unauthorized changes, bit rot

### **AVAILABILITY** 🚀
*Having what you need when you need it.*  
- **Technical:** Redundancy, backups, load balancing
- **Human:** DR plans, maintenance windows, capacity planning
- **Failure Mode:** DDoS, ransomware, "oops I deleted the database"

**#OG INSIGHT:** *"The triad is a balancing act. Maximum confidentiality kills availability. Perfect availability risks integrity. Your job is finding the sweet spot."*

## **RISK MANAGEMENT: THE ART OF WORRYING PROFESSIONALLY** 🎯

### **THE RISK EQUATION (SIMPLIFIED)**
```
RISK = Threat × Vulnerability × Asset Value
RESIDUAL RISK = What's left after you implement controls
RISK APPETITE = How much danger your executives can stomach
```

### **REAL-WORLD RISK SCENARIOS:**

**Pickpocketing Analysis:**
- **Threat:** Skilled pickpocket
- **Vulnerability:** Wallet in back pocket
- **Asset Value:** $200 cash + cards
- **Control:** Front pocket + situational awareness
- **Residual Risk:** Still possible, but harder

**Unauthorized Money Transfer:**
- **Threat:** Insider threat or hacker
- **Vulnerability:** Weak access controls
- **Asset Value:** Company funds + reputation
- **Control:** Segregation of duties, approval workflows
- **Transfer:** Cyber insurance policy

## **GOVERNANCE: THE BUREAUCRACY THAT SAVES YOUR ASS** 📜

### **THE HIERARCHY OF CONTROL:**
```
LAWS/REGULATIONS (GDPR, HIPAA) → You MUST do this
     ↓
STANDARDS (NIST, ISO) → How you SHOULD do it  
     ↓
POLICIES (Company Rules) → What we WILL do
     ↓
PROCEDURES (Step-by-step) → How we ACTUALLY do it
```

### **PRIVACY VS SECURITY:**
- **Security:** Protecting systems and data from threats
- **Privacy:** Controlling who can see personal information  
- **Overlap:** Good security enables privacy, but they're different goals

**#OG REALITY CHECK:** *"GDPR isn't about being nice—it's about not getting fined 4% of global revenue."*

## **AUTHENTICATION: PROVING YOU'RE YOU** 🔑

### **FACTORS OF AUTHENTICATION:**
- **Something you know:** Password, PIN
- **Something you have:** Phone, token, smart card  
- **Something you are:** Fingerprint, face, retina
- **Somewhere you are:** GPS location, IP address
- **Something you do:** Typing pattern, behavior

**MFA = Any combination of 2+ factors**

### **WHY MFA MATTERS:**
```
Single Factor: "I know the password" → Vulnerable to phishing
Multi-Factor: "I know + I have" → Much harder to compromise
```
