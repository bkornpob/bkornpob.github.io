**Technical vs Logical Access Controls** 🔐👥

*Let's dive deep. Quietly.* 🤿🔇

---

## **TECHNICAL CONTROLS** ⚙️🔧
*The digital enforcement mechanisms - code that says "no"*

**Examples:**
- Firewalls 🧱
- Encryption 🔒  
- Access Control Lists (ACLs) 📋
- Intrusion Detection Systems 🚨
- Biometric scanners 👁️
- Smart cards 💳

**#OG Translation:** *"The systems that automatically enforce the rules"*

---

## **LOGICAL CONTROLS** 🧠⚖️  
*The policy and procedural frameworks - the rules themselves*

**Examples:**
- Password policies 🔑
- Security awareness training 🎓
- Least privilege principle 📉
- Separation of duties 👥
- Background checks 📋
- Data classification 🏷️

**#OG Translation:** *"The human-readable rules that the technical controls implement"*

---

## **THE EXPLOIT'S PERSPECTIVE** 🕳️🔍

**Vulnerability:** Thinking they're separate systems  
**Patch:** They're interdependent layers

*Technical controls without logical controls =* **misconfigured enforcement**  
*Logical controls without technical controls =* **unenforced policies**

**Your encryption is cute, but your policy is weaker.** 😏🔓
