# NMAP = **ACTIVE** RECON 🎯🔥

**Why?** 🤷‍♂️

**Passive Recon** 👻📡
- **You:** Just *listening/watching* 👂
- **Examples:** `whois` 🌐, `theHarvester` 🛸, Shodan search 🔎
- **Target:** Doesn't know you exist 🫥
- **Like:** Stalking someone's social media 📱

**Active Recon** 💥🔊  
- **You:** *Touching/knocking* on doors 🚪👊
- **Examples:** `nmap` 🗺️, `nc` 🔌, `enum4linux` 🪟
- **Target:** Sees your connection in logs 📝👀
- **Like:** Actually ringing their doorbell 🔔

**NMAP is LOUD** 🔊🎺
- Sends packets directly to target 📨➡️🎯
- Shows up in firewall logs 🧱📊
- Target can see they're being scanned 👁️🕵️

*Passive = you watch them 👀 | Active = they feel you 🤚* 😏🔓
